Registering a business is often treated as a single event — a form submitted, a certificate received. In practice, it is the first step in a much longer process of building a proper corporate foundation.

Business registration establishes the legal existence of a company, but the structures, governance, and compliance obligations that follow are what determine whether that foundation can actually support growth.

This is why registration should be approached with the business's future needs in mind, not just its immediate legal requirements.
