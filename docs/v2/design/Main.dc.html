Our process

Every engagement follows the same six steps.

Nothing starts until the scope is agreed in writing, and nothing reaches you before our management team has reviewed it.

  1. 01EnquiryTell us what you need. We respond within 24 hours.
  2. 02Understand the needA Discovery Call, or the Quarterly Business Check-Up for Partners.
  3. 03ProposeA written proposal: scope, deliverables and what is not included.
  4. 04AgreeWork starts only once the proposal is signed.
  5. 05DeliverVetted specialists do the work. A reviewer checks every deliverable first.
  6. 06Hand over and closeYou accept in writing. Files are handed over and access removed.
