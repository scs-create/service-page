Annual returns and statutory filings

We prepare and submit the returns and updates the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) expects after registration, and keep your company records in step with them.

Service ID CR-01 · Delivered in person in Abuja or remotely, wherever you are

Who it is for

Companies, business names and incorporated trustees registered in Nigeria, including foreign-owned companies whose owners manage the business from abroad.

Why it matters

Good standing

Banks, investors and partners check your status on the public register.

Records that match

Your directors, shareholders and addresses read the same everywhere.

One person to ask

A named coordinator tracks every filing until the CAC responds.

What you receive

  • The filed return or update, with the CAC acknowledgement
  • Updated statutory registers that match what was filed
  • A compliance calendar of your next filing dates
  • A short filing record: what was filed, when and by whom

What you provide

  1. Your CAC registration number (RC, BN or IT number)
  2. Current directors, shareholders or trustees, and any changes since the last filing
  3. Financial statements, where the return requires them
  4. Your written authority for SCS to file on your behalf

How it runs

The same six steps as every SCS engagement: enquiry, understanding the need, a written proposal, agreement, delivery with management review, and written acceptance. Our process →

What this service does not include

  • A promise of when the CAC will process a filing, or of its decision
  • Tax returns, which sit under Tax registration and compliance
  • Audited accounts, which come from your accountant or auditor
