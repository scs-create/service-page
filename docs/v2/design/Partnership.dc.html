SCS Partnership

One accountable coordinator for your organisation.

An SCS Partnership is a retainership plan agreed with each partner. It covers your recurring corporate, filing, documentation and operational support, with projects added as they are diagnosed and agreed.

What partners receive

Every plan is built around the partner. These come with every partnership.

Quarterly Business Check-Up

A structured review each quarter that names up to three priorities.

SCS Business Growth Report

The findings and recommendations, written down for your board or team.

One coordinator

A named person who knows your organisation and answers for every task.

Scoped projects

Each new project agreed in writing before work starts, with a review before delivery.

Who it suits

  • Growing companies

    Recurring filings, documentation and operations, handled in one place.

  • NGOs and institutions

    Governance records and donor-compliance support, year round.

  • Foreign-owned companies

    A coordinator on the ground in Nigeria, working in your time zone.

  • International firms

    A remote operations partner in Nigeria (see below).

Are we a fit?

A partnership works when it works for both sides.

What we look for

  • A willingness to share the information a diagnosis needs
  • A named contact on your side
  • Work we can deliver well

What you can expect from us

  • A neutral diagnosis, even when the answer is to defer or decline
  • Scope, deliverables and exclusions in writing
  • Vetted specialists with a backup, and a review before delivery
  • Service levels that are defined and kept

If we are not the right fit for a need, we will say so.
